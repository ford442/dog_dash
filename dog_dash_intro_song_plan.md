# Dog Dash — Intro Song Plan

**Project:** Dog Dash (side-scrolling whimsical space flight game)  
**Tool:** Minimax Audio Music  
**Song Type:** Main Title / Intro Theme (plays on title screen + first 30–45 seconds of gameplay)

---

## 1. Overall Vision

A bright, magical, heart-warming intro song that instantly communicates:
- "You're about to go on a joyful space adventure with a cute dog"
- Whimsical + adventurous + slightly dreamy
- Kid-friendly but not childish — appealing to all ages

**Core Emotion:** Joyful wonder + gentle excitement

---

## 2. Musical Style & References

**Primary Style:**  
Modern whimsical adventure music with music-box + soft synth elements

**Key Influences:**
- Studio Ghibli (Spirited Away, Howl's Moving Castle) — magical, warm, twinkly
- Celeste (Chapter 1 & 2) — uplifting, emotional, pixel-perfect feel
- Undertale (True Lab / Waterfall) — gentle, memorable melody
- Modern cozy games (Stardew Valley, Spiritfarer) — soft synths + acoustic warmth

**Genre Tags for Minimax:**
`whimsical adventure`, `magical music box`, `soft synth harp`, `uplifting indie game soundtrack`, `pastel space`, `cute but epic`

---

## 3. Technical Specifications

- **Length:** 1:45 – 2:15 (loopable version preferred)
- **BPM:** 92–98 (gentle but with forward momentum)
- **Key:** C Major or G Major (bright and friendly)
- **Time Signature:** 4/4 with light swing feel
- **Structure:**
  1. 0:00 – 0:18 → Gentle intro (music box + soft pads)
  2. 0:18 – 0:45 → Main melody enters (warm synth lead)
  3. 0:45 – 1:15 → Build + countermelody (harp + light strings)
  4. 1:15 – 1:45 → Climactic section with gentle choir/pads
  5. 1:45 – end → Beautiful fade or seamless loop point
